## Opening title (opening-title)

Create a motion study called “Opening title”.

GOAL
Introduce the idea with a confident, brief title, then return to the presenter. Use this treatment for: Opening a topic or chapter. Interpret the idea creatively; a pixel-perfect copy of any existing demo is not required.

FORMAT AND STYLE
Make a 6-second, 16:9, 1920×1080 composition at 30 fps. Export a silent preview. Use a clean editorial style, generous spacing, and readable sentence-case type. Palette: navy #004472, blue #0076BD, pale blue #66A4D9, light gray #E6E7E8, and white; optional restrained yellow #F3DE89 accent. Use Arial Nova or Arial. Keep important content inside approximately 80-pixel safe margins. Use smooth, restrained easing and clear reading holds.

SCENE AND SAMPLE COPY
Open on a white title canvas with the headline “Start with the destination” and smaller subtitle “A clearer way to tell your story.” Use generous negative space and one blue accent rule. Finish on full-frame presenter footage.

ANIMATION BEATS
0–0.6s: draw the accent rule, then settle the headline upward with a soft fade. 0.6–2.8s: hold the title readable. 2.8–3.4s: use a clean directional wipe into the presenter. 3.4–6s: hold the presenter.

ASSETS
Use the presenter clip I attach or supply. Keep its natural proportions and continuous playback. If no clip is available, use a clearly labeled presenter placeholder and make its media input easy to replace.

ADAPTATION
Keep the headline short; let it read before returning to the speaker. Make these controls easy to change: Title, subtitle, alignment, hold, entrance and exit. Demo timings and copy are illustrative; replace them with actual spoken cues when adapting to a finished video.
